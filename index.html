AFI Circular No. 88/09-2026 — Official Rules & Notification

Official communication from Athletics Federation of India (AFI) · 09 September 2026

OFFICIAL AFI NOTIFICATION

Circular No. 88/09-2026

Date: 09 September 2026 · Issued by: Athletics Federation of India (AFI)
To: All Affiliated State Units & All Affiliated District Units
Competition Dates
14 – 16 November 2026
Woxsen University, Kamkole, Sadasivpet, Hyderabad
In collab. with Govt. of Telangana
Subject: 20th National Inter District Junior Athletics Meet 2026 (NIDJAM)

1. Age Brackets & Cut-Off Dates (as on 16 Nov 2026)

  • Under 14 Years (Boys & Girls): Born between 17/11/2012 & 16/11/2014.
  • Minimum Age Limit: Minimum 12 years and above as on 16 November 2026.
  • Under 16 Years (Boys & Girls): Born between 17/11/2010 & 16/11/2012.
  • Mandatory Age Verification: 13 November 2026 at meet venue by AFI Age Verification Committee including Doctors.
  • Proof of Age: Birth Certificate issued by "Birth & Death" Registration Office of Municipal Corporation / Nagar Palika / District Office, OR 10th Standard Examination Certificate.
  • Overage Penalty: Athletes found overage forfeit T.A., food, and accommodation.

2. District Quotas & Boarding Limits

  • Boarding & Lodging: AFI provides free boarding and lodging to a maximum of 13 Athletes and 2 Officials per district.
  • Hospitality Window: Food provided from 13 to 17 November 2026.
  • U-14 Category District Quota: Each district can send maximum 4 athletes (boys and girls together).
  • U-16 Category Limit: Maximum 2 athletes per event from the same district.

3. Online Entry & Mandatory AFI UID

  • Online Entry Portal: Entries must be submitted online on AFI Website: www.indianathletics.in.
  • Mandatory AFI UID: Every athlete must register on the AFI Website to obtain an AFI UID via https://ors.indianathletics.in/#/login.
  • Digital Certificates: Issued strictly as per the name registered in the online entry.
  • Entry Window: 15th October 2026 to 30th October 2026 (strict deadline).

4. Eligibility, Rail Fare & Declarations

  • District Meet Mandatory: Only districts that conducted their official District Athletics Meet are eligible.
  • Mandatory Documents: 1. Results of District Meet · 2. Press-cuttings · 3. Photographs of the Meet · 4. District Association PAN Number.
  • Rail Fare Reimbursement: Paid ONLY to the bank account of the District Athletics Association (no personal accounts).
  • Parent / Guardian Undertaking: Written declaration from parents/guardians regarding safety and liability is mandatory.

Complete Original Circular Text (Verbatim): 
Circular No. 88/09-2026
09 September 2026

To,
All Affiliated State Units
All District Units affiliated to State Units

Subject: 20th National Inter District Junior Athletics Meet 2026 (NIDJAM)

Dear Sir / Madam,

I am now pleased to inform you that the 20th edition of National Inter District Junior Athletics Meet will be held at Woxsen University, Kamkole, Sadasivpet, Hyderabad, Telangana, in collaboration with Government of Telangana from 14 – 16 November 2026.

Eligibility: Only those District Athletics Associations, which have conducted their District Athletics Meet, are eligible to participate. The districts units are required to send the following documents along with the online entry:
1. Results of the District Meet
2. Press-cuttings
3. Photographs of the Meet
4. Only the districts who has a valid PAN Number on the name of their respective District Athletics Association will be eligible to send entries.

Issuance of Certificates: Digital certificates will be issued as per the name mentioned in online entry.

Number of Events an Athlete can participate:
In case of U-16 group an Athlete can participate in one event and in same event maximum of two athletes can be entered. In case of U-14 an athlete can choose any one of the triathlon groups and the Kids Javelin Throw will be mandatory for all the U-14 athletes.

Events to be conducted in NIDJAM 2026 are as under:

UNDER 14 YEARS (BOYS/GIRLS)
S. No.   EVENTS           DESCRIPTION
1.       Triathlon(A)     60m, Long Jump (5m approach), High Jump (Scissor)
2.       Triathlon(B)     60m, Long Jump (5m approach), Back Throw (1kg Shot put)
3.       Triathlon(C)     60m, Long Jump (5m approach), 600m
4.       Kids Javelin Throw with five-meter runway will be mandatory for all the U-14 athletes

Age Brackets:
Boys & Girls U-14 years - Born between 17/11/2012 & 16/11/2014
Boys & Girls U-16 years - Born between 17/11/2010 & 16/11/2012

Minimum Age Limit for Participation:
The minimum age limit for participation in age group of U-14 is 12 years and above as on 16 November 2026. Boys and Girls under 12 years are not eligible to participate.

Procedure of Sending Entries:
Entries are to be filled ONLINE on AFI Website www.indianathletics.in.
For Online Entry, every athlete has to register on AFI Website and has to obtain AFI UID. To obtain AFI UID, athlete has to submit their clear Photo, Date of Birth Proof & Address Proof and other details. The AFI UID can be obtained through the following link: https://ors.indianathletics.in/#/login
For ONLINE entry, each District Unit will be provided Login ID & Password and ONLINE entry will remain open from 15th October 2026 to 30th October 2026. The procedure to send the ONLINE ENTRY will be shared separately in due course.
In U-14 category each district can send maximum 4 athletes (boys and girls together).
Only Online Entries will be accepted. Entries will not be accepted on WhatsApp or Email or any other mode.

Last Date of Entry:
The last date of receipt of Entries "ONLINE" is 30th October 2026.

For any enquiry, please contact:
1. Mr. C.S. Rathi, Coordinator, Athletics Federation of India
   Mobile Number: +91 9313167978

Age Verification:
AFI Age Verification Committee including Doctor(s) will be present to assess the age of athletes. It may please be noted that no athlete will be allowed to participate if he/she is not cleared by the Committee. It is therefore the responsibility of the Managers/Coaches to ensure that they present their athletes before the Committee immediately after arrival at the venue.
It is mandatory to produce original proof of date of birth. Please note that Age Verification will be done on 13th November 2026.

Proof of Age:
The athletes are required to produce the Birth Certificate issued by "Birth & Death" Registration Office of Municipal Corporation/Nagar Palika/District/ 10th Standard Examination.

Food and Accommodation:
Food will be provided from 13 to 17 November 2026.

Re-imbursement of Rail fare:
Rail fare will be reimbursed on verification of original rail tickets & actual participation of athletes in the competition. A photocopy of the rail tickets is required to be submitted along with reimbursement form. The reimbursement of the rail fare will be only made in the bank account of the District Athletics Association. (No personal account details will be accepted).
All those athletes found overage will not be entitled to get T.A/food/accommodation etc. No District Athletics Associations will be allowed the entry of its athletes, if District Meet has not been conducted as per the guidelines of AFI.
AFI is providing free boarding and lodging to maximum of 13 Athletes and 2 Officials per district.

It may please be noted that AFI will not be responsible for any mishap/incident during the period of stay of the athletes/officials at the venue of the Meet. The District Secretaries may, therefore, obtain written undertaking/declaration in this regard from the parents/guardians of athletes selected for participation in NIDJAM, 2026 and send the same to this office along-with entry.

All State Units are requested to circulate this communication to their affiliated District Units at the earliest and ensure timely submission of entries to enable smooth conduct of the Championship.

Regards,
Bahadur Singh Sagoo
President
