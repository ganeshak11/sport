Circular No. 88 Guidelines

🪪
AFI UID Mandatory Every athlete must have an AFI UID from ors.indianathletics.in before online entry.
🏥
Age Verification (13 Nov 2026) AFI Medical Committee will verify original Birth Certificate (issued by Municipal Corporation / Nagar Palika) or 10th Standard Certificate.
🎯
Event Rules • U-14: 1 Triathlon + Kids Javelin (Mandatory)
• U-16: Exactly 1 event (max 2 athletes per event from Mysuru).
🚆
Rail Fare & Boarding AFI provides free boarding/lodging for up to 13 athletes and 2 officials (13–17 Nov). Rail fare is reimbursed only to Association account.
Zero Tolerance on Over-aging: Any athlete found overage by the AFI Committee will be immediately disqualified, and all T.A., food, and accommodation privileges will be forfeited.
