ATHLETICS FEDERATION OF INDIA (AFI)
20th National Inter District Junior Athletics Meet 2026 (NIDJAM)
Circular No. 88/09-2026 (Dated: 09 September 2026)
Official Strict Rules & Compliance Guidelines for Mysuru District Athletics Association
================================================================================

1. GENERAL MEET DETAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Championship: 20th National Inter District Junior Athletics Meet 2026 (NIDJAM)
- Venue: Woxsen University, Kamkole, Sadasivpet, Hyderabad, Telangana
- Collaboration: Government of Telangana & Athletics Federation of India
- Competition Dates: 14 – 16 November 2026
- Age Verification Date: 13 November 2026 (at competition venue)
- Boarding & Lodging Window: 13 to 17 November 2026
- Official Contact: Mr. C.S. Rathi, Coordinator, AFI (+91 9313167978)

2. DISTRICT ELIGIBILITY & DOCUMENT MANDATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only District Athletics Associations that have conducted their official District
Athletics Meet as per AFI guidelines are eligible to participate.
Mandatory district submission requirements with online entry:
1. Complete Results of the District Meet
2. Official Press-cuttings of the District Meet
3. Photographs of the District Meet
4. Valid Permanent Account Number (PAN) registered in the name of the District
   Athletics Association (personal PANs are not accepted)
5. Reimbursement of Rail Fare will be paid ONLY into the verified official bank
   account of the District Athletics Association upon verification of original
   rail tickets and actual participation.

3. QUOTA RESTRICTIONS (STRICT PER-DISTRICT LIMITS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Overall District Contingent: Maximum of 13 Athletes and 2 Officials eligible
  for free boarding and lodging provided by AFI.
- Under 14 (U-14) Quota: Maximum 4 athletes per district (boys and girls combined).
- Under 16 (U-16) Quota per Event: Maximum 2 athletes can be entered in the
  same event from the same district.

4. AGE BRACKETS & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (CUTOFF DATE: 16 NOVEMBER 2026)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Under 14 (U-14) Years (Boys & Girls):
  * Eligible Date of Birth: Born between 17/11/2012 and 16/11/2014 (inclusive).
  * Minimum Age Requirement: Must be 12 years and above as on 16 November 2026.
  * Athletes under 12 years of age are strictly NOT eligible.
- Under 16 (U-16) Years (Boys & Girls):
  * Eligible Date of Birth: Born between 17/11/2010 and 16/11/2012 (inclusive).
  * Must be 14 years and above as on 16 November 2026.
- Proof of Age Documents (Mandatory for Physical Verification on 13 Nov 2026):
  * Original Birth Certificate issued by "Birth & Death" Registration Office of
    Municipal Corporation / Nagar Palika / District Office, OR
  * 10th Standard Examination Certificate.
  * Athletes found overage or cleared by the AFI Medical & Age Verification
    Committee will be disqualified and forfeit all T.A., food, and accommodation.

5. ATHLETE PARTICIPATION & EVENT RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. UNDER 14 YEARS (BOYS & GIRLS):
   - An athlete can choose any ONE of the following Triathlon groups:
     * Triathlon (A): 60m, Long Jump (5m approach), High Jump (Scissor only)
     * Triathlon (B): 60m, Long Jump (5m approach), Back Throw (1kg Shot Put)
     * Triathlon (C): 60m, Long Jump (5m approach), 600m
   - Kids Javelin Throw (with 5-meter runway) is MANDATORY for ALL U-14 athletes.
   - Total events per U-14 athlete: Exactly 1 Triathlon group + Kids Javelin Throw.

B. UNDER 16 YEARS (BOYS & GIRLS):
   - An athlete can participate in EXACTLY ONE event.
   - Permitted Events:
     1. 60m (Boys & Girls)
     2. 80m Hurdles (Boys: 0.838m | Girls: 0.762m)
     3. 600m (Boys & Girls)
     4. High Jump - Scissor Only (Boys & Girls)
     5. Long Jump - 5m Approach (Boys & Girls)
     6. Shot Put - Standing (Boys: 4Kg | Girls: 3Kg)
     7. Javelin Throw - 10m Approach (Boys: 600gms | Girls: 500gms)
     8. Pentathlon:
        - Boys Pentathlon: 60m, 80mH (0.838m), Long Jump (5m Approach), Shot Put (Standing 4Kg), 600m
        - Girls Pentathlon: 60m, 80mH (0.762m), Long Jump (5m Approach), Shot Put (Standing 3Kg), 600m

6. ONLINE REGISTRATION & AFI UID PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Portal URL: Entries must be submitted online on AFI Portal (www.indianathletics.in).
- Mandatory AFI UID: Every athlete must possess a valid AFI UID.
  AFI UID is obtained at: https://ors.indianathletics.in/#/login
- Clear Photo, Date of Birth Proof, and Address Proof are required for AFI UID.
- Digital Certificates: Issued strictly based on the athlete's name in the online entry.
- Online Entry Timeline:
  * Opens: 15th October 2026
  * Closes: 30th October 2026 (23:59:59 IST)
  * No entries accepted via WhatsApp, email, or physical post.

7. PARENT / GUARDIAN UNDERTAKING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AFI and District Associations are not liable for any mishap or unforeseen incident
during the event and transit. A signed written declaration/undertaking from the
parent or legal guardian is mandatory for all selected athletes.
